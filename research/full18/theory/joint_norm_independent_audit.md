# Independent audit: joint norm constructive attempt

## Verdict

The construction in `joint_norm_constructive_attempt.md` passes this bounded mathematical audit. It gives an exact per-sample complex-algebraic compatibility encoding, denominator localization, and conditional reconstruction of latent ray roots. It gives **no further reduction of the shared optical unknowns and no constructive breakthrough in the missing efficient full18 inverse**. The draft states that stopping verdict correctly.

## Verified claims

1. **Rank-32 tower and matrices.** Successive monic quadratic extensions are free of rank two, so the five-root algebra is free of rank 32 without separability assumptions. The recursive block multiplication formula and successive quadratic norm identity are correct, including nonreduced specializations.

2. **Joint compatibility.** For a fixed numerical specialization over C, the common right kernel of the two multiplication matrices is nonzero exactly when the ideal generated by the two screen residuals is proper. If the ideal is proper, a local component containing it has a nonzero socle annihilated by both residuals. If it is the whole algebra, the identity is a multiplication-operator combination of them, so their common kernel is zero. Maximal ideals over C give the common tower points.

3. **At most 33 norm tests.** The determinant of M_gx + lambda M_gy is the product, over the finitely many tower points, of gx(p) + lambda gy(p), counted with local algebra lengths. It vanishes identically exactly when one point annihilates both residuals. Degree at most 32 makes 33 distinct fixed evaluations equivalent to all coefficient equalities. Separate scalar norms do not ensure a common branch.

4. **Localization.** The stable ideal image(M_T^32) is precisely the sum of local components where T is nonzero, hence is the localized algebra with its own identity. Multiplication by T is invertible there. Restricted screen-coordinate matrices therefore give the stated denominator-safe compatibility test. The empty algebra gives determinant 1 and no compatible point. The draft correctly retains changing rank and basis strata; globally, the restricted inverse formulas are rational on those strata rather than one fixed polynomial matrix family.

5. **Conditional reconstruction.** A one-dimensional common kernel is preserved by every root multiplication operator. Their scalar actions satisfy the tower equations and recover the joint point by any nonzero vector-coordinate ratio. This does not require a reduced point. For real matrices the line admits a real generator. Higher-dimensional kernels must be retained and resolved, not rejected.

## Additional verified ambient test

Let G be the stacked residual multiplication matrices and let K = ker G. A compatible point with T nonzero exists exactly when M_T^32 K is nonzero, equivalently

rank([G; M_T^32]) > rank(G).

Indeed, K is invariant under all multiplication operators. The zero-T local components are killed by the indicated power, while multiplication by T is invertible on the surviving components. This avoids selecting a localized basis for a fixed-parameter compatibility test; it does not eliminate the shared parameters.

## Scope and stopping condition

Complex compatibility is not physical admissibility. Every original reality, positive-root, forward-normal, axial, strict traversal, and native-bound condition remains necessary. Kernel reconstruction assumes the shared parameter trial is already supplied. Neither the small instantaneous algebra nor the norm pencil establishes a known-coefficient speed eigenproblem, a fixed linear shift propagation, an identifiable small coefficient model, or manageable global recovery from 200 samples.

The appropriate conclusion is to stop this bounded attempt at an exact representation, with the essential shared-optical recovery step still unsolved. This audit is a mathematical verification, not proof-assistant formalization, a numerical campaign, a runtime guarantee, or a literature-novelty assessment.
