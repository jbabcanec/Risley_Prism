Exact-model harmonic balance continuation (2026-10-02)

This is an optional numerical proposal, not a solution of the global inverse.
It preserves the full18 physical map, native box, 200 timed positions and all
four geometry variables. Spectral generators and every initialization come
from the supplied observations. No optical Taylor coefficient is an input.

Let U have orthonormal columns spanning sampled sine/cosine lattice lines,
including DC, formed from an observed spectral speed proposal. P=UU^T. The
stage transform is W_rho=P+rho(I-P), with rho>0, applied separately to x/y.
We fit W_rho(F(theta)-y), with F evaluated by exact paired-face Snell transfer.
Four affine geometry coordinates are projected in this same weighted norm.
The derivative includes the residual-dependent variable-projection term.
Final completion and selection use all original unweighted sample positions.

The algebra is exact: ||W_rho r||_2^2=||Pr||_2^2+rho^2||(I-P)r||_2^2.
Its singular values are 1 and rho, so positive rho discards no sample-space
direction. At rho=1 the method is the original least-squares problem. Thus
this transform supplies no additional information; any advantage must be
numerical conditioning or basin entry and must be measured against a common
completion budget. It cannot remove an actual optical ambiguity.

For hard per-coordinate observation error eta, every transformed coordinate
has allowance eta times the absolute row sum of the transform. Reporting eta
unchanged after weighting would be incorrect. All final residual checks use
the untransformed observations, so the implemented proposal does not make
that substitution. Projection-only readout is separately diagnosed: given
Q=U^T, its exact hard-noise bound is eta*sum_k |Q_jk| on coordinate j.

Pure projection onto DC and the three signed fundamental frequency pairs
has at most 7 scalar basis functions per axis, hence at most14 real observed
coordinates. For a fixed proposed basis it cannot support a locally injective
smooth full18 inverse on an open18-dimensional physical box. Intermodulation
and higher harmonics, or the complementary raw data, are therefore essential.
This statement concerns the fixed projection; it does not assume that finite
record projections equal exact infinite-time optical Fourier coefficients.

The code never truncates F into a polynomial or an assumed finite Fourier
series. Higher optical modes can contribute through finite-window leakage,
and this same contribution is present in the transformed exact prediction.
The measured complementary residual of each record is reported as omitted
sample-space content. It is not a certified uniform Fourier-tail bound.
