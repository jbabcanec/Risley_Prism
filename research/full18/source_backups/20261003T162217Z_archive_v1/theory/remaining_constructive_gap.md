# Remaining constructive gap: certifying useful global localization

This note records the scope after the reduced compiler, exact boundary compactification, conditional all-branch continuation theorem, whole-prior spectral outer-cover results and certified adaptive inverse synthesis. It introduces no empirical claim. Measurement error remains the symbolic parameter \(\epsilon\); all eighteen native parameters remain unknown.

## What is established mathematically

- The coupled-vector model has an exact, ambiguity-complete finite-sample inverse under the declared rational clock, finite exact input encodings and sampled-time strict physical conditions. Complete real-algebraic decomposition retains singular, disconnected and positive-dimensional fibers.
- A coupled index chart reduces the forward compiler to five roots and instantaneous interval-atom degree at most 896. Native indices must be reconstructed jointly with the beam coordinates; the original diagonal native-coordinate conversion does not transfer unchanged. Invertible source transport gives exact-data two-distance fibers, while positive noise retains a shared two-component anchor error. Explicit positive circuits preserve mixed-strict affine feasibility and threshold endpoints.
- The symbolic-error family gives exact consistency and unacceptable-error thresholds, with endpoint status. Eighteen native diameters at most .002 are the sharp criterion for an unrestricted .001 point-estimation guarantee. Regular interior branches have sharp LP sensitivity coefficients and valid quadratic bounds; singular envelopes admit fractional powers, floors and jumps.
- For the original product-box chart, every forward direction/position denominator has a uniform positive lower bound on the weak transmitted branch. Hierarchical inward wedge scaling proves that the explicit weak set \(W\) is exactly the closure of the strict sampled-time physical domain \(P\). The forward map has a finite continuous semialgebraic extension to compact \(W\). Weak boundary points are genuine limits, but are not physical solutions when an original strict constraint is zero.
- Away from a compact projected boundary-plus-critical discriminant, the eighteen-output projection is a proper finite-sheet covering. Complete anchor enumeration, a whole projected noise cube avoiding the discriminant, and a certified singular-value lower bound give an all-branch candidate cover. The theorem now exists conditionally; those certificates have not been instantiated for an identified coupled full-vector record.
- A global forward continuity modulus with a safe \(1/8\)-Hölder bound permits LP-dual exclusion at critical refraction. The new joint observation/traversal violation LP gives an explicit continuity modulus and conditional quantitative adaptive counts. It removes the previously missing rate for changing traversal relaxations; it does not establish useful record-dependent constants or total runtime.
- Exact mixed-strict geometry elimination followed by a one-coordinate query gives symbolic native-envelope construction in at most sixteen outer variables. A prescribed finite adaptive phase followed by exact completion of every unresolved frontier piece gives a terminating ambiguity-complete inverse under finite exact encodings, without genericity or conditioning assumptions.

The compactification, its quantitative modulus and the projected candidate balls use the original product-box chart. Transferring them to the reduced coupled index chart requires explicit coordinate bounds. Physical validity between samples remains a separate requirement.

## What the conditional cover still requires

For a particular record and allowance, a practical all-branch certificate must construct or certify the projected discriminant, show that the **entire** projected noise cube avoids it, enumerate **every** anchor root, and bound the inverse-conditioning margin \(\eta\) over the whole preimage of that cube. For a coordinate selector, the resulting chart balls have radius at most \(\sqrt{18}\epsilon/\eta\), or corresponding branch-dependent bounds. Their number and radii must be useful; existence of a positive margin alone does not establish this.

Lifting one fitted root is not complete anchor enumeration. Filtering only the anchor fiber is not a noisy inverse. Every sheet over the full projected observation set must be retained and checked against all 400 original residual inequalities. Chart balls must be converted to native ranges with the appropriate inverse chart. A projected chamber can contain several sheets, and degree one alone does not exclude additional sheets of opposite orientation.

The exceptional set, prior faces, critical-normal and traversal endpoints, zero wedges, collisions and other singular strata remain part of the inverse. If the noise cube meets the discriminant, the covering theorem is inapplicable there; this neither proves inconsistency nor permits deleting those systems. They require another verified enclosure or the complete algebraic backend. Coordinate selection preserves the sample-local compilation structure; arbitrary dense projections coupling samples require their own complexity accounting.

The remaining practical question is whether these certificates can be produced at manageable cost and yield a small, useful cover for relevant records. The theorem has not established that the discriminant is easy to construct, anchor enumeration is cheap, \(\eta\) is large enough, or the number of sheets is small.

## Certified adaptive completion and its remaining performance gap

For fourteen original optical coordinates \(x\) in the compact weak optical domain \(D\), the joint LP minimizes over the compact four-variable geometry box the largest of all 800 signed observation violations and 1200 negative traversal margins. Its value \(v_\epsilon(x)\) is nonpositive exactly when a compatible weak geometry exists. The bound
\[
|v_\epsilon(x)-v_\epsilon(x')|\le\Psi(\|x-x'\|_\infty),
\qquad \Psi(r)=\max\{\omega_{\mathrm{fwd}}(r),\Phi(r)\}\le Cr^{1/8}\quad(0\le r\le1)
\]
uses the same geometry in the forward and traversal moduli and remains valid at critical normals. Certified convex guarded neighborhoods can instead supply a Lipschitz bound. Explicit simplex-dual weights give reusable affine lower bounds in \(\epsilon\), with strict exclusion intervals and separately justified mixed-strict endpoint tests. Neither \(v=0\) nor a weak optical root supplies strict physical feasibility automatically. The new LP requires no Hoffman bound or rate for a changing geometry relaxation.

Unanchored mixed-strict circuits eliminate geometry exactly. Appending one queried original coordinate gives at most fourteen reduced optical variables, \(\epsilon\), and the queried value: **sixteen outer variables**. Each retained optical cell carries its parameter-dependent convex geometry fiber. Original indices use their positive joint reconstruction equations; their extrema cannot be recovered from separate reduced-coordinate extrema. Eighteen such decompositions and fixed-dimensional graph processing give exact native endpoints and diameter decisions. There are \(O(K^5)\) candidate supports, each using at most five sample traces, and compiled degree \(O(K)\) with large fixed constants. The single-sample degree 896 is not the cross-sample circuit degree. Exact algebraic field degrees, embeddings, isolating data and coefficient heights must be charged; an uncharged compositum of all observations is invalid. These improvements do not make wholesale circuit enumeration or the exact backend inexpensive.

The fast path remains a sound enclosure while any frontier piece is unresolved. After a prescribed finite work budget, exact cell/fiber completion of every such piece supplies unconditional mathematical termination and the exhaustive outcomes: inconsistency, nonempty all-eighteen target recovery, or actual strict ambiguity at that target. Spectral fits, sparse supports discovered so far and selected local continuations are not exhaustive by themselves. No practical end-to-end implementation or runtime follows from this termination theorem.

Quantitative adaptive counts require a relatively open semialgebraic retained region \(U\) containing the **entire weak compatible optical set**, with validated enclosures of every compatible geometry above it. Given certified inner-cover distance \(b>0\), joint-violation gap \(\gamma>0\), and \(\Psi(h)\le Ch^\alpha\), the stopping scale is
\[
h_* = \min\{1,b,(\gamma/(4C))^{1/\alpha}\},
\qquad N=O((1+D_0/h_*)^{14}).
\]
The crude gap dependence is \(\gamma^{-112}\) globally for \(\alpha=1/8\), or \(\gamma^{-14}\) on a certified Lipschitz region. This \(\gamma\) is a joint observation/traversal violation gap; comparison with an earlier screen-residual gap needs another geometry conditioning bound. Exact domain-emptiness, containment and algebraic-anchor queries, LP arithmetic and encoding growth remain costs beyond the node count. The count is for fixed \(\epsilon\); reusable dual intervals do not guarantee uniform bounds across noise transitions.

A smaller output-sensitive count needs further record-dependent certificates after normalizing the root-box diameter to one: ball-cover numbers \(B_r(K_\epsilon)\le Mr^{-s}\) for nonempty \(K_\epsilon\), \(0\le s\le14\), and an error bound \(\max(v_\epsilon,0)\ge a\,\operatorname{dist}(x,K_\epsilon)^\beta\) with certified \(a>0\) and rational \(\beta>0\). The resulting exponent is \(p=s+(14-s)\max\{0,1-\alpha/\beta\}\), giving \(O(Mh_*^{-p})\) nodes for \(p>0\), or \(O(M[1+\log(1/h_*)])\) for \(p=0\), with constants and coarse levels charged. Finite enumerated candidates allow \(s=0\); continua need validated covers or atlases, and positive-noise interiors may have \(s=14\). Pointwise full rank and dimension alone supply none of the needed useful global constants. A small replayable tree for an informative record remains an implementation hypothesis, not an established success.

## What the whole-prior spectral cover adds and leaves open

The first-order annihilator gives an exact polynomial description of a guaranteed speed outer cover on the entire admitted prior. In the three speed half-angle coordinates, its 200-sample description has **772 scalar polynomial inequalities of degree at most six**, together with the speed bounds. It needs no supplied true frequencies, mode separation or nonzero amplitudes. Zero speeds, collisions, signs and prism assignments are retained. With a certified uniform first-order tail allowance \(\tau_1\), the score condition \(S_y(v)\le\epsilon+\tau_1+\gamma\) contains every compatible speed triple; \(\gamma>0\) gives the stated strict exterior residual gap. This is an outer cover, not an exact description of the speeds of all compatible physical systems.

Its usefulness is unresolved. The strict critical-limit witness forces every global absolute first-order tail bound above 17,600 native screen units, and the stated orders two through four still require absolute bounds above 17,000. Because the normalized score is at most \(\|y\|_\infty\), the absolute-tail test admits the entire speed prior whenever \(\|y\|_\infty\le\epsilon+\tau_m\). Existence of a finite whole-prior Taylor bound consequently does not supply useful localization or a useful noise threshold. These are analytical witness claims from the supporting memos, not new experiments or local executions of their proof scripts.

A valid improvement bounds the **filtered** tail directly. For a real annihilator of the relevant Taylor trace, normalized to coefficient \(\ell^1\) norm one and including the constant node, the positive and negative coefficient masses are each one half. A certified instantaneous optical modulus therefore gives filtered remainder at most \(\tfrac12\omega_{\mathrm{inst}}(d_L(v))\); it may be combined with the absolute bound by taking their minimum. The instantaneous-coordinate modulus needs its own certified constants: the existing fourteen-coordinate forward modulus cannot simply be substituted unchanged. This bound recovers the zero-frequency limit, so the large absolute-tail witness does not establish failure of filtered localization.

The triple-critical construction establishes a sharp \(1/8\) exponent for the specified **standard full-lattice Taylor/Prony filters of orders one through four** on the unguarded full prior, even along the stated family with distinct nonzero modes. Their annihilator degrees are 7, 25, 63 and 129; order five already has degree 231 and supplies no annihilation window for 200 samples. The lower bound excludes a uniform better exponent for these filters, including selection among these four orders. It is not a lower bound for every filter, structured higher-order method or exact nonlinear inverse, and it supplies no evaluated practical error threshold.

Spectral localization alone treats three speed parameters. Recovering all eighteen still requires certified coefficient and geometry regions, DC-contamination control, all compatible branches, and verification of the full physical residual conditions. A useful filtered speed cover may assist those steps; it does not replace their completeness certificates.

## Boundary and exclusion obligations after compactification

Axial and incoming-normal grazing cannot cause forward-denominator blow-up in this bounded model. Critical outgoing normal factors may still vanish. Derivatives through a critical square root and the inverse source-transport matrix can consequently be ill-conditioned or divergent. The global Hölder modulus handles forward continuity there; it supplies neither a uniform Jacobian inverse nor uniformly useful precision.

An exterior residual gap can now be posed as an ordinary minimum on a compact subset of \(W\). It follows if the retained open region contains the full weak compatible set. Checking only strict exact roots can miss a weak boundary near-alias and does not establish that hypothesis. Equality at the consistency or exclusion threshold still requires the original strict feasibility test. Weak minimizers identify limiting uncertainty, not automatically attainable physical systems at that threshold.

The guarded derivative-based order-fourteen covering estimate remains a conditional count, not a total-cost bound. The joint violation LP now strengthens the earlier qualitative weak-traversal convergence argument to the explicit boundary-safe rate and counts stated above. It does not require a positive critical-radicand margin, but useful radii, cover gaps, oracle costs and arithmetic bounds still need certification. A geometry relaxation can erase a screen-residual gap; no unproved quantitative comparison with the joint violation gap is assumed.

The data-dependent critical-margin theorem now supplies a direct final-prism test. With \(u_*=\tan18^\circ\) and \(M_k(\epsilon)=u_*\sqrt{(|y_{k,x}|+\epsilon)^2+(|y_{k,y}|+\epsilon)^2}\), every compatible weak or strict system obeys
\[
\frac{R_3(k)}{Z_3(k)}\ge\max\!\left\{0,\frac{50-M_k(\epsilon)}{53}\right\}.
\]
Thus \(\max_k M_k(\epsilon)<50\) certifies a positive final normal ratio throughout the compatible set without fitting or an assumed small spectral tail. The memo gives a stricter proposed-margin test, its exact symbolic error interval and sharper first-two-sample support bounds. This is a conditional statement for arbitrary fixed data: it neither proves compatibility nor establishes the record's generating model. It also gives no margin for either earlier prism by itself.

Backward certification through already guarded downstream prisms reduces each upstream stage/sample to sixteen exposure inequalities over the chosen optical superset. Positive denominator clearing gives degrees at most five for stage two and eight for stage one **in the local ray-graph variables**, with the physical graph equalities and guards retained. These are not degree bounds after elimination into native parameters. Explicit polynomial positivity identities can certify the inequalities; constructing them and proving their signs over the entire compatible optical domain remain computational obligations. No bounded search degree, universal success or cheap sign test is proved.

Conversely, a verified full-record weak critical witness with residual \(r_0\) proves that every allowance \(\epsilon>r_0\) admits strict physical systems with arbitrarily small corresponding normal factor, by the established strictification theorem. A positive uniform margin is then impossible for that record and allowance. At \(\epsilon=r_0\) a separate endpoint check remains necessary. A single strict near-critical witness retains only its specified target set; it does not alone rule out every positive margin. For a given record the rigorous outcomes are exclusion with a certified guard, retention with the requisite witness, or unresolved critical regions. None has yet been instantiated here for an identified coupled full-vector record.

## Nonuniqueness, implementation and publication gaps

Universal unique recovery on the full prior is false. Zero-wedge and centered gauges remain exact obstructions. The off-axis finite-record collision construction further separates local full rank from stable finite-noise recovery: its admissible amplitude cutoff and quantitative constants remain unevaluated, and its bad midpoint records do not establish ambiguity for every observed record.

No practical end-to-end complete 200-sample implementation or useful all-eighteen precision certificate has been executed here. Remaining work includes validated optical/compiler arithmetic, useful filtered-tail constants, exact or enclosed derivatives and continuity bounds, discriminant and complete-root certification, verified LP/Farkas certificates, full-cover bookkeeping, exceptional-stratum handling and all-residual native-range computation. No numerical admissible-error threshold for a coupled full-vector observation record is supplied.

The bounded Lean support has **nineteen** checked declarations across two independently audited packages, each compiled successfully without warnings under Lean 4.33.0. The original eleven verify transport and spatial algebra, conditional critical/flight signs, and the scalar square-root inequality. Eight further declarations prove the conditional final-prism observation-margin implication from explicit ray, traversal, slope and residual premises. These checks do not formalize the complete optical model or derive all model-level premises; they also do not formalize the noise-cutoff formula, upstream exposure tests, compactification, three-prism modulus, covering theorem, compiler degree bound, collision or spectral constructions, adaptive inverse synthesis, or full inverse. The recorded standard foundational axioms are not custom optical assumptions; full-model proof-assistant verification is not claimed.

The identified original-protocol record (`observations.json`, `cases[0]`, `random_00`) is a **canonical independent-axis synthetic** 200-pair record. No coupled full-vector or hardware 200-pair record was verified in the inspected files. This is a scoped provenance gap, not a claim that no such record exists elsewhere. The canonical record can support a canonical study or be treated as arbitrary fixed data whose full-vector consistency must first be tested; its labels and sample clock do not establish a full-vector generating truth. A targeted full-vector recovery certificate still needs an identified record and its error/encoding contract, followed by the requisite consistency and completeness certification.

The original clock/error contract, implementation and data validation, physical validation, literature positioning and independent expert review remain publication obligations. The research is not declared complete or publication-ready. The current theory-first scope does not authorize numerical campaigns. Original Dropbox files and observations remain unchanged.
