Complementary multiple-shooting investigation, 2026-10-02

Implemented and executed two routes on the original passive 200-position
protocol. Every native parameter stays unknown over its original full bound.
No measurement planes, parked rotors, derivatives, known glass, known speeds,
known geometry or true initialization are inputs. These are noiseless numerical
development tests of convergence, not finite-noise or global recovery proofs.

1. Shared-hardware block-phase continuation

For block b(k), replace the exact rotor phase temporarily by

  gamma_i(k) = 2*pi*N_i*t_k + ay_i*pi/180 + delta_i,b(k),  delta_i,0 = 0.

All native hardware and all three speeds remain common to every block. Exact
Snell propagation is retained within each block. Eight blocks introduce 21
temporary phase variables. The four affine coordinates (d,g,px,py) are solved
jointly inside their full boxes, leaving 35 nonlinear optimization variables.
This is a reduced multiple-shooting parameterization of 39 total unknowns,
rather than 1212 independent full-record ray variables.

The objective combines scaled optical residuals, branch penalties, and
lambda times adjacent phase-slip differences. Lambda follows .01,.1,1,10,100.
Every temporary slip is then discarded and all18 are fitted to the original
200-point common-clock model. Candidate acceptance always uses that final
model, not the relaxed data residual. Screening covers all six physical orders,
two nominal material/geometry scales, independent pencil and FFT proposals,
and observation-derived repeated-generator/cancellation proposals. Nominal
starts are guesses, not supplied calibration; all coordinates are released.

Controls: zero-slip transfer agrees exactly with the previously verified
poc3 affine model; complex-step reduced Jacobian agrees with an independent
directional finite difference to relative error 3.0208959425e-9; a block slip
affects only its own observations. See controls.json and controls.py.

Four predeclared development cases, scored only after observation-only fitting:

moderate_unsorted    8.9783e-9              5.9097e-23              10.88
random_03           3.9876e-11             5.9837e-24              11.65
exact_collision     20.8562                1.4842e-5               23.81

Three meet the original .001 native-error criterion. The weak-first routine
still labels its result unresolved because its numerical max-residual gate
is 1e-8. This acceptance label and true native-coordinate error are separate.
The collision remains a failure; it is not a physical impossibility claim.

2. Explicit constrained ray-state comparison

Use 40 original observation nodes: the first eight consecutively, followed
by 32 dispersed nodes. Nonuniform differences preserve the exact clock and
avoid imposing one uniformly coarsened sampling lattice. Six physical-order
initializations feed the original sparse trust-constr implementation, with
all rotor equalities explicit. The 252 variables and 234 rotor equalities
retain eighteen local degrees of freedom when regular. Reconstructed native
candidates are ranked on the full200 record and receive exact-clock VarPro
polishing. No coarse-node result is accepted directly.

This route meets .001 on zero of the four development cases. Final native
errors are 9.9066,8.5814,23.4372,21.0412 in the order above; full-record MSEs
are 3.1288e-6,.0116388,8.8480e-5,1.5981e-6. Times are 38.30,31.00,33.27,22.05s.
These are bounded local failures, not a universal comparison of the methods.

The coarse accepted states already show large inconsistency: the best
full-record-ranked coarse candidates have rotor defects approximately
.00115,.152,.0174,.00697 and reconstructed-normal closure defects
.00110,.217,.0286,.00740. Their lifted data fit therefore is not a coherent
physical trajectory. Source inspection establishes that the optimizer omits
all constraint Hessians and uses a damped data Gauss-Newton Hessian. It is
reasonable to investigate curvature/restoration, but these trials do not
prove that omission alone caused failure.

3. Separate failures and one adaptive diagnostic

The first block method was frozen before reading the separate combined-policy
failure observations (source/input hashes in frozen_combined_contract.json).
Its result on combined random_02 is MSE1.67255, max residual3.08895; on combined
random_03 MSE72.06998, max residual24.55815. Neither is a new recovery. These
names refer to the separate combined batch, not the development random_03.
No combined-batch truth file was read by this investigation.

The relaxed screening picked three repeated-generator candidates, suppressing
the distinct pencil/FFT proposals. An adaptive follow-up retained one finalist
from each family. Combined random_03 still failed, MSE70.20005. Thus preserving
families alone does not fix it. The original frozen failures remain preserved.
phase_blocks_v1.py retains the exact initial implementation; phase_blocks.py
adds only the optional family-diverse finalist policy.

4. Mathematical findings and limits

The source initializer excludes repeated generators from its combinations
search and creates a split repeated-line proposal only when a component is
declared missing. A collision with visible nonlinear harmonics can evade that
trigger. This motivates unconditional repeated-generator hypotheses; it does
not prove which hypothesis is true. Independent observation-derived repeated
starts were included here, with all three speeds immediately free.

Full row rank of the rotor constraint Jacobian does not establish optical
identifiability, a useful inverse condition number, or global convergence.
The original paper proves that rank without requiring distinct speeds.

For the exact-collision development case, the continuation reduced temporary
phase slips to about 2.3e-6 radians, yet its relaxed MSE remained about6.9e-5
and final exact-clock MSE1.48e-5. Restoring rotor coherence is therefore not
sufficient to escape the selected wrong hardware basin in this test.

The new phase route provides an additional usable candidate generator, but
does not improve the separate difficult cases. Its spectral proposal set and
screening still limit recovery. It should not replace the better existing
order/VarPro/low-frequency routes merely because its formulation is different.
