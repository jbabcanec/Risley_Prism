## Working title
Exact Parameter Recovery for Multi-Prism Risley Systems by Frequency-Lattice
Inversion, with Information-Theoretic Certificates

## The story in one paragraph
The scan pattern of a P-prism Risley system is a quasi-periodic signal whose
frequency module is the rank-P integer lattice generated by the SIGNED prism
speeds. Working with the analytic signal z = x + iy, every quantity the
inverse problem needs is a property of that lattice model: the generators are
the speeds (sign included), the phases of the fundamental lines are the wedge
orientations, their amplitudes are monotone in the wedge magnitudes, and the
weakly-identifiable glass/geometry/beam parameters are pinned by the exact
time-domain model once the lattice stage places the iterate in the correct
basin. Recovery therefore needs no enumeration of sign combinations, frequency
triples, or angle grids. Where recovery is impossible, the SAME lattice fit
yields Fisher bounds that certify impossibility quantitatively (pair
resolution, prism detectability, lattice degeneracy) and prescribe the
observation time that would cure it.

### III. Structure theory  (rewrite core)
- Thm 3.1 (lattice support): away from TIR, z(t) = sum_{k in Z^3} c_k
  e^{2 pi i (k.N) t}, absolutely convergent, coefficients decaying
  geometrically in |k|_1.  TEST A4: B=4 projection residual med ~4e-4.
- Prop 3.2 (signed fundamentals & leak inequality): the k = e_i line
  dominates the k = -e_i line; paraxial proof (leak ratio =
  |Kx-Ky|/(Kx+Ky) < 1 since both axis gains are positive).
  TEST A2: med leak/main, violations count.
- Prop 3.3 (phase readout): arg c_{e_i} = ay_i + 180[ax_i<0] + O(nonparax).
  TEST A3: median error ~1-2 deg.
- Prop 3.4 (amplitude law): |c_{e_i}| = a_i tan ax_i + b_i tan^3 ax_i +
  O(tan^5).  TEST A6: inversion error, true and nominal geometry.
- Prop 3.5 (finer-lattice lemma / why residual selection fails): if
  Lambda(B') contains Lambda(B) restricted to observed lines, the LS residual
  of B' is <= that of B. Hence basis selection MUST use arithmetic
  parsimony/coverage, not fit residual. (This is the honest theory behind
  three failed solver generations — documentable.)
- Identifiability: keep rank-18 local theorem + kappa hierarchy; ADD
  Def (spectral identifiability): conditions
    (i)  pair resolvability: min |N_i +- N_j| > 3 sigma_N(T)
    (ii) prism detectability: |c_{e_i}| > 5 sigma_c(T)
    (iii) lattice non-degeneracy: min_{k != e_j, |k|_1<=4} |k.N - N_j| >
          3 sigma(T)
    (iv) TIR fraction below mask budget
  Thm 3.6 (certified recovery): under (i)-(iv) the pipeline recovers all 18
  parameters; the TRF covariance s^2 (J^T J)^{-1} bounds the error.
  TEST: certify.py battery (coverage, tightness).

### IV. Algorithm  (all new)
- 4.1 De-glitch (TIR mask; self-consistent remask).
- 4.2 CLEAN line growth at order 1 (novelty preference); matrix pencil.
- 4.3 Basis selection by amplitude-weighted integer coverage (+ consensus
  reweight); alias handling.
- 4.4 Lattice VarPro: ridge + merge protections (each justified by a
  documented failure mode), guarded GN, sharp final pass, sign test,
  fundamental canonicalization (rank-extension; one extraction only).
- 4.5 Angles from the spectrum; cubic amplitude calibration (2 evals/prism);
  beam from DC; nominal-geometry bootstrap for 18-D.
- 4.6 TRF completion + verified ladder (each rung deterministic, accepted
  only by pattern-MSE certificate).
- 4.7 Certificates (success: 3-sigma bounds; failure: (i)-(iv) with
  required-T formulas from sigma ~ T^{-3/2} scaling).
- Complexity: zero forward evals for speeds; 6 for angle calibration;
  one TRF (~2k evals). Wall-clock ~1-3 s/case.
